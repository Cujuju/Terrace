## How it is made

Rebuilt in Blender from the first-party procedural temple (`plugins/temples/client/temple.ts`); maps are procedural paint. Build record: `.census/temple/` and `.census/building-kit/README.md`.

Concept images for the kit were generated and kept only as references; no generated pixels are used as texture maps.
