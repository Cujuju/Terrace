## How it is made

Built by `tools/blender/build_war_boat.py` from traced reference curves in `.boat-ref/`; see the script header.

The hull lines were traced from schematic drawings of the Gokstad ship
(`.boat-ref/gokstad_schematic.jpg`, `gokstad_section.jpg`) as measurements only;
no drawing pixels are in the model. The source and licence of those two images
are not recorded here.
